★ Since 1983 • The Culinary Pioneer

Four Decades of Gracious Dining & Heritage

The Salt’n Pepper is Pakistan’s most recognized and distinguished hospitality brand with a history of market innovation, culinary passion, and excellence in restaurant chain operations.

Chapter I • The Beginning

The Mall Road Genesis (1983)

The first Salt’n Pepper Restaurant was established on the historic Lahore Mall Road in 1983 by entrepreneur and hotelier Mahmood Akbar, widely regarded as the pioneer restaurateur of modern Pakistan.

Prior to Salt'n Pepper, family-friendly modern dining with immaculate hygiene standards and courteous continental service was nearly non-existent in Pakistan. Salt’n Pepper transformed the culinary landscape, setting benchmarks that inspired an entire industry.

"We did not merely open a restaurant; we created a dining sanctuary where families celebrate memories across generations." — Mahmood Akbar
Chapter II • Revolution

The Village Live Buffet Revolution (1992)

In 1992, Salt'n Pepper established the groundbreaking Village Restaurant on M.M. Alam Road Lahore. It revolutionized South Asian gastronomy by introducing the concept of a "Live Bazaar Buffet", where dozens of regional dishes are prepared live in front of diners in authentic clay tandoors.

With seating for over 300 guests, The Village has hosted international dignitaries, royalty, and heads of state — including the late Princess Diana and Prime Minister Benazir Bhutto.

Chapter III • National Footprint

Expansion Across the Nation

Following decades of unprecedented success in Lahore, Salt'n Pepper introduced its national franchise programme. Beginning with a prestigious flagship in Blue Area, Islamabad (2012), followed by Kohinoor City, Faisalabad (2016), and Model Town, Bahawalpur (2018).

Today, our multi-tiered formats — including Salt'n Pepper Express — bring delicious quick-service gourmet cuisine to premium malls and urban hubs nationwide.
